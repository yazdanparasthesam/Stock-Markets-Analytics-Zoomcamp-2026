# SMA Zoomcamp 2026 — Module 5 Homework README

**Topic:** Three exploratory research ideas for capstone / future strategies (no code required).
**Deliverable:** [`homework5_solutions.ipynb`](homework5_solutions.ipynb) — three free-text answers in the required six-part structure, plus a final "how the three differ" table.

## The three ideas at a glance

| # | Name | Market / universe | Mechanism | Horizon |
|---|------|-------------------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **MDAX German-language disclosure lag** | ~50 German MDAX mid-caps (XETRA) | English-only news pipelines miss German ad-hoc disclosures for under-covered names; drift after German-only news | 30–60 days |
| 2 | **EU semi-equipment lead-lag pairs** (ASML → ASMI / BESI) | 3 Euronext Amsterdam semi-cap names | Staggered earnings calendars + coverage imbalance make ASML's guidance a leading indicator for the smaller names; pair-trade until the laggard reports | 2–15 days |
| 3 | **Steam + Twitch launch-engagement nowcast** | Public Western game publishers (EA, TTWO, UBI, CDR, THQ, FDEV, PDX) | Real-time concurrent players / viewership beat sell-side consensus by 4–8 weeks; long/short vs gaming ETF after day 14 | 30–45 days |

## Why these are meaningfully different

* **Different markets:** German mid-caps vs. Euronext semis vs. global gaming publishers.
* **Different mechanisms:** language/coverage lag vs. supply-chain earnings lead-lag vs. alt-data demand nowcast.
* **Different data types:** regulatory filings/news text vs. prices + earnings calendars vs. alternative time-series from Steam/Twitch.
* **Different horizons** (2–15 days, 30–45 days, 30–60 days) so they don't all make the same macro bet.

## Structure each answer follows

1. **Market and scope** — universe + horizon
2. **Investment thesis** — what is expected to happen and why
3. **Potential edge** — why it's overlooked / slow to be priced
4. **Data and sources** — what's verified vs. still TBD
5. **Research approach** — concrete first test (intentionally starts simple)
6. **Validation and risks** — benchmarks, reject criteria, realistic failure modes

Each answer explicitly states what would cause me to reject the idea (i.e. they are *research proposals*, not "this is a guaranteed-profit strategy" pitches).
